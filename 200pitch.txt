You know that feeling in week one — sitting alone in J03 on the 9th floor, scrolling the same "how do I make friends at uni" thread on Reddit for the third time? That's the gap unipixel fills.

It's a web app built for one campus at a time. You sign up with your uni email, build a profile in under a minute, and see who's actually online right now — filter by faculty, age, MBTI, whatever matters to you. Add people, chat instantly, or hit "Find a group" and get dropped into a chat with people who are free at the same time you are.

Here's the part that gets people to put their phone down during the demo: every chat doubles as a game lobby. Tile Rush, Tic-Tac-Toe, a Gartic-Phone-style drawing game — real multiplayer, built from scratch, running inside the same window you're already chatting in. No downloads, no third-party bot bolted on.

It's not a dating app, and it's not a study-buddy spreadsheet. It's the thing that gets two strangers from "we're in the same building" to "we're actually friends," without either of them having to make the first awkward move.

Built at Syncs Hack 2026, by people who wanted this exact thing to exist.
