## Production notes

- **Pacing check:** ~230 words of VO across 3:00 leaves plenty of room for the screen-capture beats to breathe — don't rush the demo footage to fit more narration in; cut narration lines before cutting demo time.
- **The games segment (1:35–2:00) is the differentiator** — if the edit runs long anywhere, protect this section first. It's the feature judges won't have seen in a "just another social app."
- **Live footage needed:** registration flow, dashboard filter interaction, a DM being sent, all three minigames in action, the match page toggles + result, profile page (avatar/bio/visibility), dark mode toggle. Consider recording each in its own clean take so the editor can trim to the beat.
- **Freeze frame/bookend:** reusing the opening J03 shot at 2:38 for the "resolution" beat is optional but a nice touch if the footage allows it — ties the story back together without needing new VO to explain it.
